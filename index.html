Quick help

Add an item: Open a room, tap Add Item, then attach photos, a receipt, value, and serial number.

Create a claim pack: Open Claim Export, review the report, and share the generated PDF or evidence ZIP.

Incident Mode: Use it after fire, theft, water, or storm damage to record event details and affected possessions.
