ClaimReady Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

ClaimReady is designed to keep your home inventory and evidence private. The app does not require an account and does not send your inventory to a ClaimReady server.

Data you create

Room names, item details, values, notes, serial numbers, photos, receipts, incident details, PDFs, and ZIP exports are created and stored on your device. They leave your device only when you explicitly use the system share sheet or another platform feature that you control.

Camera and photo access

ClaimReady requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to attach evidence. These permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

ClaimReady does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal data.

Backups and third-party services

Your device settings may cause app data or shared exports to be included in Apple backups or stored in a destination you choose. Those services are governed by their own privacy terms.

Data deletion

You can delete individual inventory records in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups or exported copies you control.

Children

ClaimReady is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13.
